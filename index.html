How it works

Store the cold,
not the current.

Cooling is 15% of the world's electricity — and lithium batteries are too expensive to store it at scale. ThermoPod takes a different path: it stores cooling directly. Harvest cheap solar or off-peak grid as cold, then release it when demand peaks. No lithium cells, no fire risk, no degradation.

01 · Harvest

Charge when energy is cheap

Run ThermoPod on solar at noon or off-peak grid at night. Cooling is stored as a phase-change — no electricity held at all.

02 · Hold

Keep it, cost-free, for hours

No self-discharge like a battery. The cold just stays, ready for the 6 PM peak or a 12-hour reefer route.

03 · Release

Deliver on demand, diesel-free

Stored cold flows precisely, from −25°C deep-freeze to ambient AC — monitored end-to-end.

The result: cold-chain reliability without the cost, complexity or carbon of diesel gensets or lithium batteries.
